Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy covers the website sunshine-sirens (this site) and "Clip Uploader", the internal application that publishes videos to the Sunshine & Sirens YouTube channel.

What Clip Uploader does

Clip Uploader uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to, and read statistics of, the Sunshine & Sirens channel, which is owned by us. It is used only by the channel owner. It does not let anyone else sign in.

Data we access

  • With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth): permission to upload and manage videos on our own channel and to read that channel's YouTube Analytics.
  • Public YouTube data (video titles, view counts, comments) used to find and credit source moments.

We do not collect, store or sell personal information about viewers or other YouTube users. Access tokens are stored encrypted on our own server and are never shared.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers.

Third parties

By using our YouTube channel you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke Clip Uploader's access to a Google account at any time at Google security settings.
